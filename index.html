01 // CLASSIFIED STORY

OBJECTIVE: ELYSIUM CORE
Mission briefing · Division 2

When Memory Becomes the Enemy

Specter Ops must locate and secure the Elysium Core, a hidden capsule containing Nexarion — a mysterious fluid connected to the source of anomaly powers. If it falls into enemy hands, the consequences could reshape reality.

The team uses Oblivion to erase the location of the Templar's Keep from Kakujo's top lieutenants. But the effect spreads to Specter Ops. Operatives begin forgetting critical details — even their own mission. Now they are racing against time and their fading memories.
