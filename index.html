01 // Mission briefing

OPERATION: ELYSIUM
Primary objective

Secure the Elysium Core

Specter Ops must locate and retrieve a mysterious capsule hidden somewhere beyond the reach of ordinary maps. The capsule contains Nexarion — a volatile fluid linked to the source of anomaly power. If it reaches the wrong hands, the balance between reality and the anomaly world may collapse.

But the team has played a dangerous wild card: using Oblivion to erase the location of the Templar's Keep from Kakujo's top lieutenants. The effect is spreading. Operatives are beginning to forget critical mission details.

A hostile wilderness where dense fog cuts visibility to roughly 10 metres. Toxic plants release hallucinogenic spores, mutated insects attack on sight, and unstable ground shifts like quicksand. Kakujo's soldiers patrol the forest.
